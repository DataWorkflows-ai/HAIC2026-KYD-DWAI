## Monthly Energy Trends

The Monthly Energy Trend provides a comprehensive statistical overview of Hawaii’s energy sector, featuring up-to-date data on gasoline prices, petroleum imports, fuel consumption, and electricity generation to track the state’s evolving energy landscape. Data are presented by island with state total.
- Monthly Energy Trend : Highlights and Summary Tables (September 2026)
- [Monthly Energy Trend : Highlights and Summary Tables (September 2026)](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/files/2026/10/Energy_Trend.pdf)
- Monthly Energy Data : Historical data from January 2006 to September 2026
- [Monthly Energy Data : Historical data from January 2006 to September 2026](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/files/2026/10/Monthly_Energy_Data_a.xlsx)
