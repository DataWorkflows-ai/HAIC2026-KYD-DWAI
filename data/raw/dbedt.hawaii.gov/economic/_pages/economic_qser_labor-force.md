## A. Labor Force & Jobs

3rd Quarter 2026 Report
Download Labor Data Tables (spreadsheet)
- [Download Labor Data Tables (spreadsheet)](https://files.hawaii.gov/dbedt/economic/data_reports/qser/A-labor-tables.xlsx)
Hawai‘i’s labor market conditions were mixed in the second quarter of 2026 compared to the same quarter of 2025. The civilian labor force and civilian employment increased modestly. Civilian unemployment and the unemployment rate also increased. Civilian non-agricultural wage and salary jobs increased in the private sector but decreased in the federal government sector compared to the second quarter of 2025.
In the second quarter of 2026, the civilian labor force averaged 691,950 people, an increase of 4,750 people or 0.7 percent from the same quarter of 2025 (Table A-1). In the first half of 2026, the civilian labor force increased 1,100 people or 0.2 percent from the same period of the previous year.
Civilian employment averaged 673,250 people in the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 2,650 people or 0.4 percent compared to the same quarter of 2025 (Table A-2). In the first half of 2026, average civilian employment increased 550 people or 0.1 percent from the same period of the previous year.
In the second quarter of 2026, the number of civilian unemployed averaged 18,750, an increase of 2,200 people or 13.3 percent from the same quarter of 2025 (Table A-3). In the first half of 2026, the number of unemployed increased 600 people or 3.6 percent from the same period of the previous year.
The unemployment rate (not seasonally adjusted) was 2.7 percent in the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 0.3 percentage points from the second quarter of 2025 (Table A-4). In the first half of 2026, the unemployment rate increased 0.1 percentage points from 2.4 percent in the first half of 2025 to 2.5 percent.
In the second quarter of 2026, Hawai‘i’s nonagricultural wage and salary jobs averaged 645,100 jobs, an increase of 2,700 jobs or 0.4 percent from the same quarter of 2025 (Table A-6). In the first half of 2026, average nonagricultural wage and salary jobs increased 0.2 percent or 1,000 jobs from the same period of the previous year.
The increase in jobs in the second quarter of 2026 was due to job increases in the private sector, offsetting a decrease in federal government jobs. The private sector added about 4,400 non-agricultural jobs compared to second quarter of 2025. The number of jobs increased the most in Health Care and Social Assistance, which gained 2,700 jobs or 3.5 percent (Table A-16); followed by Construction, which added 1,300 jobs or 3.2 percent (Table A-7); Accommodation, which gained 800 jobs or 2.0 percent (Table A-18); Food Services and Drinking Places, which gained 600 jobs or 0.9 percent (Table A-19); and Information which added 300 jobs or 4.5 percent in the quarter (Table A-12).
The Government sector lost 1,700 jobs or 1.3 percent (Table A-21) in the second quarter of 2026 compared to the same quarter of 2025. The decline was in the Federal Sector, which lost 2,900 jobs or 8.2 percent (Table A-22). On the other hand, the State Government added 500 jobs or 0.7 percent (Table A-23) and the Local Government added 700 jobs or 3.6 percent (Table A-24), compared to the second quarter of 2025.
