Note: Under Size of Firm, multi-establishment and multi-industry employers are counted for each worksite reported. Under Size of Employer, each employer, as registered with Hawaii’s Unemployment Insurance Division, is counted in the primary worksite. For example, a restaurant with locations in Honolulu and Maui under the same Unemployment Insurance account will be counted under Honolulu and Maui on the Size of Firm table, but counted just once in the primary worksite with the consolidated employment of all locations on the Size of Employer table.
