# County Data Portal – Research & Economic Analysis

Source: https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/county-data-portal/

(Collection of dashboards and data pages with county-level data)
Return to the dashboard main page
- [Return to the dashboard main page](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/dashboards-main/)
Population data Latest population estimates, long-term trends, and characteristics of population and housing, and links to migration information sites
- [Population data](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/population-dashboard/)
Economic overview Annual trends and performances of Hawaii economy based on GDP, personal income, CPI, and industry level job and earning data
- [Economic overview](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/economy-dashboard/)
Agriculture sector Summary of Hawaii agriculture sector based on the data from Census of Agriculture and annual farm activity data from BEA
- [Agriculture sector](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/agriculture-dashboard/)
County highlights Monthly summary of COVID-19 metrics and various economic activities in the counties (Discontinued in August 2023)
- [County highlights](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/county-economic-conditions/)
Business data County-level business data by 6-digit NAICS industry, from County Business Patterns (CBP)
- [Business data](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/business-data/)
Small area data ACS 5-year estimates for all Census Bureau geographies, with detailed data down to the census tract level
- [Small area data](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/small-area-data/)
