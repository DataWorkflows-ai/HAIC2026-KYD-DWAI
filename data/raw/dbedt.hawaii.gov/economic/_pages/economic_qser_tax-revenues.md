## C. Tax Revenues

3rd Quarter 2026 Report
Download Tax Data Tables (spreadsheet)
- [Download Tax Data Tables (spreadsheet)](https://files.hawaii.gov/dbedt/economic/data_reports/qser/C-tax-tables.xlsx)
State general fund tax revenues decreased in 2025 compared to 2024. 1/ While the general fund tax revenue data for the first and second quarters of 2026 were not available at the time of publication, second quarter 2026 data were available for several of its components.
Comparing these components in the second quarter of 2026 to the same quarter of 2025, General Excise and Use Tax (GET) revenues, Net Corporate Income Tax revenues, and Transient Accommodations Tax (TAT) collections increased, while Net Individual Income Tax revenues decreased.
In the fourth quarter of 2025, total tax collections distributed to the State general fund totaled $2,338.4 million, an increase of $7.3 million or 0.3 percent over the same quarter of 2024 (Tables C-1 and C-2). In 2025, State general fund tax revenues decreased $546.2 million or 5.4 percent from the previous year.
During the second quarter of 2026, GET revenues (excluding the county surcharges) totaled $1,222.2 million, an increase of $22.8 million or 1.9 percent over the same quarter of 2025 (Table C-3).
Compared to the second quarter of 2025, Net Individual Income Tax revenues decreased by $3.0 million or 0.3 percent to $1,012.4 million in the second quarter of 2026 (Table C-4). In the second quarter of 2026, Declaration of Estimated Taxes increased $3.5 million or 0.8 percent (Table C-5),
Payments with Returns increased $2.3 million or 0.9 percent (Table C-6), Revenues from Withholding Tax on Wages decreased $5.7 million or 0.9 percent (Table C-7), and Refunds increased $3.1 million or 1.1 percent (Table C-8).
Net Corporate Income Tax revenues, which tend to be volatile by nature, were $264.9 million in the second quarter of 2026, a 20.5 percent increase compared to the $219.9 million in the second quarter of 2025 (Table C-9). In the second quarter of 2026, Declaration of Estimated Taxes increased by $46.5 million or 21.7 percent (Table C-10), Payments with Returns increased $7.0 million or 35.9 percent (Table C-11), and Refunds increased $8.4 million or 62.8 percent (Table C-12), compared with the same quarter of 2025.
In the second quarter of 2026, TAT revenues increased $21.7 million, or 10.7 percent, compared to the same quarter of 2025 (Table C-13).
According to the most recent data available, in the first quarter of 2026 the Retailing tax base increased $362.4 million or 2.9 percent (Table C-14), the Services tax base increased $145.7 million or 2.5 percent (Table C-15), the Contracting tax base increased $11.2 million or 0.3 percent (Table C-16), and the Hotel Rentals tax base increased $80.2 million or 3.9 percent (Table C-17), compared to the same quarter of the previous year.
—————
1/ When interpreting tax figures in conjunction with other quarterly data in this report, it should be kept in mind that the tax data represent collections during the quarter. The transactions on which the taxes were paid did not necessarily take place during the quarter.
