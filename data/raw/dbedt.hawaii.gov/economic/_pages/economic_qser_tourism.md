## D. Tourism

3rd Quarter 2026 Report
Download Tourism Data Tables (spreadsheet)
- [Download Tourism Data Tables (spreadsheet)](https://files.hawaii.gov/dbedt/economic/data_reports/qser/D-tourism-tables.xlsx)
Domestic visitor arrivals increased while international visitor arrivals decreased in the second quarter of 2026. The domestic average daily census and international average daily census decreased. Nominal visitor expenditures increased compared to the same quarter of 2025.
The total number of visitor arrivals by air increased 22,985 or 0.9 percent in the second quarter of 2026, compared to the second quarter of 2025 (Table D-2). The total average daily census decreased by 20,388 or 8.9 percent in the quarter. In the first half of 2026, total visitor arrivals by air increased 108,681 or 2.2 percent, while the average daily census decreased 9,090 or 3.9 percent from the previous year.
In the second quarter of 2026, total visitor arrivals on domestic flights increased 25,957 or 1.2 percent compared to the same quarter of 2025 (Table D-3). In the first half of 2026, domestic arrivals were up 113,274 or 2.8 percent from the previous year.
Arrivals on international flights decreased 2,972 or 0.9 percent in the second quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter of 2025 (Table D-4). In the first half of 2026, international arrivals decreased 4,593 or 0.6 percent from the previous year.
In terms of major market areas, from the second quarter of 2026 to the same period of 2025, arrivals from the U.S. West increased 2,235 or 0.2 percent (Table D-5), arrivals from the U.S. East increased 76,295 or 12.5 percent (Table D-6), and arrivals from Japan increased 7,924 or 5.1 percent (Table D-7). In the first half of 2026, arrivals from the U.S. West were up 26,175 or 1.0 percent; arrivals from the U.S. East were up 168,593 or 13.4 percent; and Japanese arrivals were up 20,115 or 6.3 percent from the previous year.
The total average daily visitor census was down 8.9 percent or 20,388 visitors per day in the second quarter of 2026, over the same quarter of 2025 (Table D-8). The domestic average daily census decreased 9.2 percent or 18,172 visitors per day (Table D-9) and the international average daily census decreased 7.0 percent or 2,216 visitors per day (Table D-10). In the first half of 2026, the domestic average daily census decreased 8,380 or 4.2 percent; and the international average daily census decreased by 709 or 1.9 percent from the previous year.
Nominal visitor expenditures by air totaled $5,505.86 million in the second quarter of 2026, an increase of $182.7 million or 3.4 percent from the second quarter of 2025 (Table D-11). In the first half of 2026, visitor expenditures totaled $11,603.5 million, an increase of $686.40 million or 6.3 percent from the same period of the previous year.
Total airline capacity, as measured by the number of available seats flown to Hawai i, increased 5.4 percent or by 175,981 seats (Table D-12) in the second quarter of 2026, domestic seats increased 8.0 percent or 200,923 seats (Table D-13), and international seats decreased 5.9 percent or 35,942 seats (Table D-14), compared to the same quarter of 2025. In the first half of 2026, the number of total available seats increased 5.7 percent or 372,386 seats, domestic seats decreased 7.4 percent or 387,820 seats, and international seats decreased 1.2 percent or 15,434 seats from the same period of the previous year.
In the second quarter of 2026, the statewide hotel occupancy rate averaged 75.3 percent, 2.4 percentage points above the same quarter of 2025 (Table D-15). In the first half of 2026, the statewide hotel occupancy rate averaged 75.9 percent, 1.5 percentage points above the occupancy rate for the same period of the previous year.
