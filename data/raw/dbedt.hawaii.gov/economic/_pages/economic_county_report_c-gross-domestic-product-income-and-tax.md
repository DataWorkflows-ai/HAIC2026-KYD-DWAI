## C. Gross Domestic Product, Income and Tax

This section presents statistics on total and per capita gross domestic products (GDP) and personal income. GDP figures are reported in both current and constant (inflation-adjusted) dollars. Personal income data includes total figures as well as specific components of state and county-level income. Additionally, this section provides data on the major tax revenues and tax bases of the Hawaii State Government.
The primary data source for these tables are the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA), the U.S. Census Bureau, and the Hawaii State Department of Taxation (DoTax). Per capita estimates presented in this section are calculated by DBEDT using the U.S. Census Bureau’s annual population estimates. Note that the Department of Taxation’s compilation of county data on a “location of payer” basis was discontinued after 2017.
Due to the Federal Shutdown, many of the federally-sourced data releases were delayed and rescheduled or cancelled. BEA’s “Gross Domestic Product by County and Personal Income by County, 2024” originally scheduled for release on December 3, 2025, have been rescheduled for release on February 5, 2026. Tables C-1 to C-12 will be updated when data become available. BEA state data updated September 26th but due to revisions for earlier years was no longer comparable to the county data. See the Quarterly Statistical & Economic Report (QSER) 2025Q4, tables B-1 to B-13 for updated state data.
Sections 6, 7, and 25 of Historical Statistics of Hawaii present earlier year data.
