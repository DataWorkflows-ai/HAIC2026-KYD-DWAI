## County Economic Conditions

3rd Quarter 2026 Report
Download County Quarterly Economic Indicators (spreadsheet)
- [Download County Quarterly Economic Indicators (spreadsheet)](https://files.hawaii.gov/dbedt/economic/data_reports/qser/7-county-tables.xlsx)
In the second quarter of 2026, all four counties experienced job growth, along with rising unemployment rates. Private building permit values declined in all counties, and visitor arrivals declined in Honolulu, Hawai‘i and Kaua‘i counties compared with the same quarter of 2025. Visitor data for Maui County is not available.
In the second quarter of 2026, the unemployment rate in Honolulu increased 0.3 of a percentage point, from 2.3 percent in the second quarter of 2025 to 2.6 percent. In Kaua‘i County, the rate also rose similarly, 0.3 of a percentage point, increasing to 2.4 percent compared with 2.1 percent during the same period in 2025. The rate in Hawai‘i County increased the most, 0.5 of a percentage point, from 2.8 percent to 3.3 percent. In Maui County, the rate increased 0.1 of a percentage point, from 2.7 percent to 2.8 percent.
In the second quarter of 2026, Honolulu gained 1,300 nonagricultural wage and salary jobs, or 0.3 percent, compared with the same quarter of 2025. The number of jobs increased the most in Health Care and Social Assistance, which added 1,900 jobs or 3.3 percent; followed by Natural Resources, Mining and Construction, which added 800 jobs or 2.8 percent. The Government sector lost 1,900 jobs or 1.9 percent, with the Federal Government losing 2,500 jobs or 7.7 percent, while State and Local Government each gained 300 jobs, or 0.6 and 2.5 percent respectively
In the second quarter of 2026, Hawai‘i County added 200 nonagricultural wage and salary jobs, or 0.3 percent, compared with the same quarter of 2025. Health Care and Social Assistance added 300 jobs or 3.7 percent, Retail Trade added 100 jobs or 1.0 percent, Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation added 100 jobs or 5.9 percent, and Natural Resources, Mining and Construction added 100 jobs or 2.4 percent. Government sector employment was unchanged.
Maui County gained 1,300 nonagricultural wage and salary jobs, or 1.8 percent, in the second quarter of 2026 compared with the same quarter of 2025. Natural Resources, Mining and Construction and Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation each added 300 jobs, or 6.1 and 15.0 percent respectively. The Government sector gained 200 jobs or 2.2 percent.
Kaua‘i County added 100 nonagricultural wage and salary jobs, or 0.3 percent, in the second quarter of 2026 compared with the same quarter of 2025. Natural Resources, Mining and Construction; Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation; and Health Care and Social Assistance each added 100 jobs, or 4.5, 8.3, and 3.7 percent respectively. Government sector employment was unchanged.
In the second quarter of 2026, visitor arrivals by air decreased 1.7 percent in Honolulu, 10.3 percent in Kaua‘i County, and 8.5 percent in Hawai‘i County compared with the same quarter of 2025. No visitor data is available for Maui County.
In the second quarter of 2026, the value of private building permits decreased in all counties. Permit values fell $368.6 million or 32.0 percent in Honolulu, $39.0 million or 18.4 percent in Hawai‘i County, and $20.7 million or 12.7 percent in Maui County compared with the same quarter in 2025. Private building permit decreased in Kaua‘i (residential only) by $3.9 million or 12.6 percent over the same period.
