## QSER Archive

Previous issues of the Quarterly Statistical and Economic Report are made available here for historical purposes only. Since many data series are frequently revised, these previous reports contain a good deal of obsolete data and should not be used as a source for current data needs.
For the most recent data, users are urged to make use of the most recent online QSER. For the most recent consistent historical series, users should consult files on the Monthly Economic Indicators page.
