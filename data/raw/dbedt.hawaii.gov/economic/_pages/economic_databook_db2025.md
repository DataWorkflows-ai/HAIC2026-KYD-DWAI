## 2025 STATE OF HAWAII DATA BOOK

This edition is the 58th compiled by the Hawaii State Department of Business, Economic Development & Tourism (DBEDT) and its predecessor agencies. The first was the Statistical Abstract of Hawaii, 1962 , issued by the Department of Planning and Research. Older Databook series are available from the Databook Archive page.
- [Databook Archive](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/economic/databook-archive/)
In a significant update for this edition, the format of the tables has been thoroughly redesigned to comply with Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requirements, ensuring the data is accessible to all users. To support these enhanced accessibility features, the data tables are now provided exclusively as Excel files (PDF formats are no longer available).
