## American Community Survey 2020

American Community Survey 2020

### 1-Year Experimental Data

Due to the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on data collection efforts, the U.S. Census Bureau was unable to release the ACS 1-year estimates as a standard product. Instead, the Census Bureau released a select number of experimental estimates for the nation, states, and the District of Columbia.
The tables can be viewed on the Census Bureau’s Experimental Data page .
- [Experimental Data page](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/experimental-data/1-year.html)
