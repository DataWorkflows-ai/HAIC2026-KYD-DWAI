# Licensing of sources used in this package

Prepared Sun Nov 15, 2026, about 5:46 PM HST, for the HAIC 2026 "Know Your District" research package in this folder. This is a research inventory of terms, not legal advice. "Republish in a public open-source repo" here means including the source data or a substantial extract in a public GitHub (or similar) repository. Derived statistics with a citation are called out separately.

**How to read the last column.** "Yes (public domain)" means U.S. federal works that are not subject to copyright (17 U.S.C. § 105) and may be included with a citation. "Yes, with attribution" means the publisher allows use if you credit them as specified. "Link, do not republish" means no license granting republication was found, so the safe path is to cite and link the official page rather than ship the publisher's files. "Restricted" means the publisher limits who may use the file.

## Package practice (recommendation)

- Include federal public-domain datasets and the derived CSVs already in this folder, with citations.
- Include state and county *derived* tables (eligibility, caseloads, the no-charter meals roll-ups, the HPHA property inventory) with a clear source URL and date, and without official seals or logos.
- Do **not** add Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, HiCentral, or UHERO data files to a public repo until attribution is applied and (for Redfin, HiCentral, and UHERO) terms are confirmed. Linking is the default for UHERO.
- Do **not** include the HUD USPS vacancy file.
- Do **not** use HIDOE or any state/county name in a way that implies official endorsement.
- Items a human should still confirm if the repo will be public and will include third-party files: Zillow and Realtor.com redistribution terms; Redfin and HiCentral terms; UHERO (no license found — link rather than republish); Honolulu ASMTGIS and Hawaii County tax bulk-data terms; HIDOE terms if any commercial use is planned.
